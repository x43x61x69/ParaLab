Saves and Backups

  • Save Backup & Restore reads or restores the connected device’s save.
  • Workspace → Save opens save tools for the loaded ROM.
  • The GARDEN in My Lab Tamagotchi is part of the device save. To write your Garden changes, turn off Keep saves and check the write summary.

Visits and Transfers

  • Playdate starts a visit with a Tamagotchi Paradise.
  • Send to My Lab Tamagotchi transfers an adult Tama to the Garden.
  • Special Food lets you choose the egg traits to send to Neon Planets.
  • Send Gift exchanges a gift. Send Lab Item lets you choose an item and edit its prices before sending.
  • Downloaded Lab Items reads the items already saved on a connected Tamagotchi Paradise.

Tamagotchi Paradise Controls

Menu and Fields
  • With the menu shortcut installed, press A and C together on the home screen. Turn the wheel to select, press B to confirm or C to go back.
  • In the ParaLab menu, use FIELD to move your Tama. Switching fields stores the current field’s save and restores the destination’s save. DNA and residents stay with their field.
  • In the six-field configuration, Orange Tropics and White Glacier can import LAND, WATER and SKY from your ROMs. Jade Forest can import SKY.
Return to the Flash Tool

On a Tamagotchi Paradise with the recovery Flash Tool installed, hold C while pressing Reset or inserting the batteries. Then reconnect in Device Console.

Device Console

Use Device Console at Your Own Risk

Incorrect wiring can damage your device. Before connecting, check the adapter pins. Before writing, back up your ROM and saves.

Connection

  1. Wire the Adapter

    Use 3.3 V UART logic. Match the adapter’s printed pin labels and leave its power pin disconnected.

  2. Prepare the Device

    Tamagotchi Paradise

    On the Planet screen, press A. Select Connect, then select Download twice.

    My Lab Tamagotchi

    Open the FLASH TOOL on My Lab Tamagotchi. Wait for the READY screen.

    Before using UART on My Lab Tamagotchi, install Flash Tool by flashing a modified update file or ROM that includes it.

  3. Choose the USB Port

    Select Connect above, then choose your USB adapter in desktop Chrome or Edge.

Start the Updated ROM

  1. Unplug the UART adapter from the top of the Tamagotchi.
  2. Press and release the Reset button on the back of the Tamagotchi once. Alternatively, remove the batteries and put them back in.

Playdate Setup

  1. Connect the USB-UART adapter with the game running.
  2. Select Start Playdate below and choose the adapter.
  3. On the device, open CONNECTION → PLAYDATE and press B.

My Lab Transfer

  1. Connect the USB-UART adapter to My Lab.
  2. Open CONNECTION → LAB INVITE. If the garden is full, choose a Tama to replace.
  3. Select Send to My Lab below and choose the adapter.
